Map for Google Photos

Privacy policy

Map for Google Photos adds a map of your photos to Google Photos on the web. This is everything it does with your data.

What it reads

While you use photos.google.com, it reads each photo's location, date, size and thumbnail address from Google Photos, through your own signed-in session, the same way Google Photos' page reads your timeline. It only reads: it never changes, uploads or deletes anything in your library.

Where it keeps it

In the extension's own storage on your computer, so the map opens quickly next time. It's removed when you uninstall the extension. There is no server: your photo data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What other services see

  • The map itself (streets, place names, the fonts for its labels) comes from OpenFreeMap, which serves OpenStreetMap data. Like any web map, your browser asks OpenFreeMap for the areas you look at, so their servers see your IP address and which parts of the map you view. Because the map opens framed on your photos and you zoom into the places you've been, those areas follow where your photos were taken. The requests carry no cookies, account details or photos, and OpenFreeMap never gets the list of your photo locations.
  • Your photos and thumbnails load from Google, exactly as they do on Google Photos.

What it doesn't do

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no accounts, and no selling or sharing of data.
